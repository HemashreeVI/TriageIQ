TriageIQ

Every customer email, ranked by urgency, with a reply ready to approve.

TriageIQ reads your support inbox, decides what needs attention first, and drafts an answer from tickets you have already solved.

  • Ranks every email by urgency and customer mood
  • Drafts the reply from your past resolved tickets
  • Sends nothing until a person approves it
Runs on Kafka, Qdrant and a local language model.
